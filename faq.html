Frequently Asked Questions

Is Earnova available to everyone?

You must be at least 18, capable of entering a binding agreement, and eligible under applicable laws and our terms.

Are earnings guaranteed?

No. Earnova does not guarantee income, commissions, sales, conversions, payout eligibility, or withdrawals.

How do I report a problem?

Use Contact Us and include enough detail for our support team to investigate.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Submit an account deletion request through Contact Us from your registered email. We may need to verify ownership and retain limited data where legally required.

What conduct is prohibited?

Spam, fake accounts, deception, impersonation, fraudulent activity, policy evasion, and attempts to compromise the service are prohibited.
