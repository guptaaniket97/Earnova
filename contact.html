Contact Us

For support, privacy requests, account deletion, or fraud reports, email support.

Before launch: replace the placeholder email in site.js with a monitored Earnova support address. This static project does not have a server-side contact form handler.

What to include

Please include your registered email, a concise description, and relevant screenshots or error messages. Never send your password, OTP, or payment credentials.

Account deletion

Send your request from the email registered to your Earnova account and state “Account deletion request” in the subject. We may verify ownership before processing and may retain limited records where required by law or for security.
